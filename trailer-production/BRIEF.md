# Meridian cinematic trailer brief

## Product

Meridian is a local, map-first study guide for all 66 chapters of Isaiah. It connects the World English Bible text with historical context, geography, language notes, source records, guided studies, and an optional Latter-day Saint reading layer.

## Audience

Readers who find Isaiah difficult to place, follow, or study in context. The audience includes individual scripture students and Latter-day Saint readers who want a clearly labeled faithful layer without losing the shared historical evidence.

## One job

Show that Meridian turns Isaiah from an isolated page into a connected place that readers can explore.

## One idea to remember

Read the text. See the world around it.

## Final action

Explore Meridian at `isaiah.josephnewelldesign.com`.

## Delivery

- Duration: 32 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 1920 by 1080
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Primary format: MP4 with stereo audio
- Editable source: this Remotion project
- Sound-off behavior: every claim and transition remains understandable without audio

## Verified claims

- All 66 chapters
- 1,292 verses
- 70 passage notes
- 1,322 curated word studies
- Local-first ordinary study with no account or network connection required
- Historical and LDS modes are separately labeled
- Source details and evidence limits stay connected to passages

The current data check is the authority for numeric inventory claims. The project README is the authority for local-first behavior and mode descriptions.

## Visual system

- Deep navy field drawn from the product interface
- Cyan for geographic and interface connections
- Warm gold for evidence, artifacts, and the final promise
- Large Georgia display type paired with Segoe UI support text
- Terrain, map routes, scripture panels, and artifact photography
- Purposeful camera pushes, route drawing, panel locks, and restrained light sweeps
- Readable holds after every major reveal

## Production method

Use deterministic Remotion scenes with local Meridian assets. Recreate supporting interface elements as editable React markup. Use the existing interface screenshot only as a product view, not as evidence for retired controls. Generate the score locally so the render has no remote asset dependency.

## Qualification rules

- Do not present approximate map paths as exact reconstructions.
- Do not present interpretive portraits or the reading landscape as archaeological evidence.
- Do not imply that the LDS mode changes the shared historical evidence.
- Do not add user, performance, or adoption claims.

## Visual proof

Render and inspect:

1. Opening hook at 1.5 seconds.
2. Map and scripture feature frame at 12 seconds.
3. Closing frame at 30.5 seconds.
4. A five-second `MapProof` composition that draws a route and locks the scripture panel beside the destination. This is the highest-risk shot because it must combine map movement, readable labels, interface detail, and a clean transition without visual overload.
