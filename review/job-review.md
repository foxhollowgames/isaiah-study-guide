# Job review

All 42 chapters and 1,070 World English Bible verses were read. The guide uses Isaiah's reader, map, chapter controls, profiles, and perspective setting.

Each chapter names its speaker. Notes distinguish the friends' claims from the opening account and the Lord's final response. Chapter 13 explains the difference between WEB and King James readings of verse 15. Church notes identify their King James reading.
