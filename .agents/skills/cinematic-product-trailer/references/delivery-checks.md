# Trailer delivery checks

## Story and claims

- The opening earns attention within the first few seconds.
- Every scene supports the trailer's one job.
- The feature sequence shows a transformation instead of listing controls.
- All product wording matches verified source material.
- The final frame gives one clear action.
- The story works without audio.

## Frame checks

- Important text stays inside safe margins.
- Text remains readable at the likely viewing size.
- No text, logos, markers, or controls are clipped.
- The hierarchy is clear in representative stills.
- Asset crops preserve the intended subject.
- Citations or limitations remain visually attached to the relevant claim.

## Motion and edit checks

- Motion has a purpose: reveal, relationship, attention, demonstration, or payoff.
- Busy moments alternate with readable holds.
- Transitions do not hide information.
- Camera moves finish before the viewer must read small text.
- Scene exits and audio cues do not stop abruptly.
- The last frame holds long enough to read.

## Export checks

- The exported file opens and plays from start to finish.
- Duration, dimensions, frame rate, video codec, and audio track match the brief.
- The first and last frames are intentional.
- There are no blank frames, missing assets, placeholder text, or network dependencies.
- Audio does not clip and ends cleanly.
- The editable source can render the same result again.
- The final path and any material limitation are reported.
